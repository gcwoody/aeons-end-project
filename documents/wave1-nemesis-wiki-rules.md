## Rageborne

### Nemesis Mat

- Wiki：[Rageborne](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Rageborne)

**Overview**

While Rageborne may not be particularly intelligent or devious, it is unmatched in strength and determination. It destroys all it can in a mad frenzy, and it's minions follow its lead wherever it goes.
Rageborne is often one of the first nemeses new players encounter, and one of the easiest to defeat. It is focused on directly attacking the mages and Gravehold, doing large amounts of damage to both. It charges up a more powerful attack, called a Strike, every time it Unleashes. When Rageborne Strikes, a card it played from a secondary deck of 6 unique powerful attacks, the strike deck.

**Setup**

Rageborne's starting life is 70.
Shuffle all of the Strike cards together and place them facedown to form the strike deck.
Rageborne gains one Fury token.

**Rule**

When Rageborne Strikes, resolve the following in order:
Draw a card from the strike deck and resolve it.
Shuffle that card back into the strike deck.
Rageborne loses three Fury tokens.
At the end of the nemesis turn, if Rageborne has four or more Fury tokens, it Strikes once.

**Unleash Effect**

Rageborne gains one Fury token.

**Increased Difficulty**

RULES: After Rageborne Strikes, it loses one Fury token instead of three.

**Clarifications & Rulings**

See here for sources.
Fury tokens are limited by their physical supply.

**Errata**

See here for sources.
In the second edition, Rageborne's setup mat should have the line: "Rageborne gains 1 Fury token". You can print out the updated mat here.
