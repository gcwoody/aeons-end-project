- Group: Tier 1
- Wiki: [Dark Conclusion](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Conclusion)
- Source template: `NemesisCard`

**Template fields**

- Type: Power
- Effect: TO DISCARD: Spend 6 Aether.
POWER 3: Unleash twice. Set this next to the turn order deck.
