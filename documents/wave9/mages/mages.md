## Indira (Beyond the Breach)

- Group: Breach Mage
- Wiki: [Indira (Beyond the Breach)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Indira_(Beyond_the_Breach))
- Source template: `Mage`

**Template fields**

- title: Light Caller
- Box: Beyond the Breach
- Complexity: 4
- Breach1: none
- Breach2: down
- Breach3: Transfer_Breach_Icon
- Breach4: none
- Unique Cards: Fused Opal
- Starting Hand: 4x Crystal
1x Spark
- Starting Deck: 4x Crystal
1x Fused Opal
- Name: Pyromancer's Guile
- Effect: Cast a spell in any ally's hand without discarding it. Then, cast it again.
OR
Gain 5 Aether that can only be used to gain a spell.
- Charge Spaces: 5
- Rules: Indira's III breach is the Transfer breach. When a spell is cast from the opened Transfer breach, that spell deals 1 additional damage and is placed into any ally's hand.

**Overview**

Following previous Indira concepts of casting spells from places other than breaches, this version of the mage can cast spells from any ally's hand with her ability Pyromancer's Guile. This combos well with her unique Transfer breach, which can place a spell cast from it into an ally's hand. Her unique card allows her to Conjure, which means she can even give allies cards other than spells when casting them as conjured spells from the Transfer breach.

**Rules**

Indira's III breach is the Transfer breach. When a spell is cast from the opened Transfer breach, that spell deals 1 additional damage and is placed into any ally's hand.

The wording on the opened breach itself is incorrect, see #Errata.

- Wiki: [Overload Stance](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Overload_Stance)
