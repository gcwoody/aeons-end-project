## The Terror in Brass

- Group: Nemesis
- Wiki: [The Terror in Brass](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Terror_in_Brass)
- Source template: `Nemesis`

**Template fields**

- Life: 99
- Box: Beyond the Breach
- Difficulty Level: 5
- Expedition Battle: 1
- Unleash: If Regeneration is in play, The Terror in Brass gains 5 life. Otherwise, advance the Worldsplitter Track.
- Increased Difficulty: RULES: Place the Worldsplitter Track into play with the increased difficulty side faceup.
- Rules: When every space on the Armor Chart is covered, The Terror in Brass is vulnerable.

When The Terror becomes vulnerable, place the Regeneration power card into play if it isn't already in play.

Double all damage dealt to The Terror while it is vulnerable.

When a player deals damage to The Terror while The Terror is not vulnerable, reduce that damage to 1.

During any player's main phase, that player may spend 3 Aether to place a nemesis token on an uncovered space on the Armor Chart and resolve its effect.

When the Worldsplitter Track is advanced, move The Terror in Brass token up one space on the Worldsplitter Track and then resolve the effect of that space.
- Setup: Place the mat with the Worldsplitter Track and Armor Chart next to this mat, normal difficulty side up.

Place a nemesis token on the "Cast any player's prepped spell" space of the Armor Chart. Place The Terror in Brass token on the Start space of the Worldsplitter Track.

Set aside the Regeneration power next to this mat faceup.

**Setup**

this card's starting life is 99.

Place a nemesis token on the "Cast any player's prepped spell" space of the Armor Chart. Place The Terror in Brass token on the Start space of the Worldsplitter Track.

Set aside the Regeneration power next to this mat faceup.

## Twice-Dead Prophet

- Group: Nemesis
- Wiki: [Twice-Dead Prophet](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Twice-Dead_Prophet)
- Source template: `Nemesis`

**Template fields**

- Life: 50
- Box: Beyond the Breach
- Difficulty Level: 6
- Expedition Battle: 3
- Unleash: Twice-Dead Prophet gains 2 life and one nemesis token.
- Increased Difficulty: SETUP: Twice-Dead Prophet gains 20 life.
- Rules: At the end of the nemesis turn, if Twice-Dead Prophet has six or more nemesis tokens, place the top card of the prophecy deck into play. Then, Twice-Dead Prophet loses four nemesis tokens.

The top card of the prophecy deck is always revealed.

When a prophecy is resolved or discarded, place it on the bottom of the prophecy deck. When a prophecy is discarded, place the card underneath it on top of the nemesis discard pile.

When a power is played off a prophecy, do not remove any power tokens from it this turn. When a minion is played off a prophecy, do not resolve its PERSISTENT: effect this turn. Twice-Dead Prophet has no maximum life.

When Twice-Dead Prophet has 99 or more life, it ascends to immortality and the players lose.

When the nemesis deck is empty, the players lose.
- Setup: Shuffle all of the prophecy cards together and place them facedown to form the prophecy deck.

When playing with 1/2/3/4 players, Twice-Dead Prophet gains 2/2/1/0 nemesis tokens.

**Setup**

this card's starting life is 50. 

Shuffle all of the prophecy cards together and place them facedown to form the prophecy deck.

When playing with 1/2/3/4 players, Twice-Dead Prophet gains 2/2/1/0 nemesis tokens.

**Rules**

At the end of the nemesis turn, if Twice-Dead Prophet has six or more nemesis tokens, place the top card of the prophecy deck into play. Then, Twice-Dead Prophet loses four nemesis tokens.

The top card of the prophecy deck is always revealed.

When a prophecy is resolved or discarded, place it on the bottom of the prophecy deck. When a prophecy is discarded, place the card underneath it on top of the nemesis discard pile.

When a power is played off a prophecy, do not remove any power tokens from it this turn. When a minion is played off a prophecy, do not resolve its PERSISTENT: effect this turn. Twice-Dead Prophet has no maximum life.

When Twice-Dead Prophet has 99 or more life, it ascends to immortality and the players lose.

When the nemesis deck is empty, the players lose.

**Unleash Effect**

Twice-Dead Prophet gains 2 life and one nemesis token.

**Increased Difficulty**

SETUP: Twice-Dead Prophet gains 20 life.

## Crooked Mask (Beyond the Breach)

- Group: Nemesis
- Wiki: [Crooked Mask (Beyond the Breach)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Crooked_Mask_(Beyond_the_Breach))
- Source template: `Nemesis`

**Template fields**

- Life: 70
- Box: Beyond the Breach
- Difficulty Level: 5
- Expedition Battle: 2;5
- Unleash: Any player gains a corruption and places it on top of their deck. That player shuffles their discard pile into their deck.
- Increased Difficulty: SETUP: Each player gains a corruption and place it on the bottom of their deck.
- Rules: * When a corruption is destroyed, place it on the bottom of the corruption deck.
* When a player would gain a corruption and the corruption deck is empty, Gravehold suffers 2 damage instead.
* At the start of each player's turn, before that player's casting phase, that player resolves all corruptions in their hand, in any order.
* At the end of each player's main phase, that player resolves all corruptions they drew during their turn.
* Corruptions may not be played at any other time.
* When a player gains a corruption, that player may not look at the corruption unless it is placed into their hand.
* Corruptions have a cost of 0 Aether when in a player's hand.
- Setup: Shuffle all of the corruption cards together and place them facedown to form the corruption deck.

**Overview**

Crooked Mask uses a secondary deck of corruption cards, which it places into the players' decks and hands, while also shuffling the players' decks. These corruption cards inflict various negative effects on the players when drawn and played.

**Changes from [[Aeon's End (Core Box)]]'s version**

* Revamped Increased Difficulty.
* Insatiable Avarice (Beyond the Breach) instructs you to "gain" a gem first before placing it into your hand, triggering any effect related to gaining (e.g. Volcanic Glass, Electinium).
* Officially recognized that corruptions have a cost of 0 Aether when in a player's hand (previously a ruling).
* Story

**Setup**

Crooked Mask's starting life is 70.

Shuffle all of the corruption cards together and place them facedown to form the corruption deck.

**Rule**

* When a corruption is destroyed, place it on the bottom of the corruption deck.
* When a player would gain a corruption and the corruption deck is empty, Gravehold suffers 2 damage instead.
* At the start of each player's turn, before that player's casting phase, that player resolves all corruptions in their hand, in any order.
* At the end of each player's main phase, that player resolves all corruptions they drew during their turn.
* Corruptions may not be played at any other time.
* When a player gains a corruption, that player may not look at the corruption unless it is placed into their hand.
* Corruptions have a cost of 0 Aether when in a player's hand.

**Unleash Effect**

Any player gains a corruption and places it on top of their deck. That player shuffles their discard pile into their deck.

**Increased Difficulty**

SETUP: Each player gains a corruption and place it on the bottom of their deck.

**Expedition Battle 5 Modifiers**

* During setup, swap one of the nemesis turn order cards for the Unleash turn order card.
* The nemesis starts with 10 additional life.
* Play using Increased Difficulty.
