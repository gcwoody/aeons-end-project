## Incorrect Content
* Indira (Beyond the Breach)'s Transfer Breach states you "may" place the cast spell into an ally's hand; however, it is not supposed to be optional. The wording on the back of the mage mat is correct.
* Oracle of Filth's setup on the back of its mat should say Abyssal Vial instead of "Whirlpool Pendant."
